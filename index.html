About

This app has info about the apps in KuuppaOS!

About TeXtpad

You can save info to TeXtpad, but it only has one textbox.
You can use it for small thoughts that you don't want to forget yet. (it saves locally to your browser!)
It's basically like notepad, but with only one note.

About Weather

This app shows you your local weather!
It shows temperature, condition (with text and an emoji) and your location.
Make sure you have enabled location services for this to work.

About Clock

This app tells you the time.
There is nothing special about this, it's just an analog clock.

About KuuppaMusic

Listen to XFM radio stream :D
Song title updates every minute.
When you minimize this app when music is playing, the song title shows in the taskbar.

About KuuppaVid

You can watch any YouTube video with this!
Just type in the video's title!

About HackCMD

With this app you can seem like you are hacking!
Just type anything in the terminal window!

About Terminal

This is just a basic terminal.
Type 'help' to see all available commands.

About Paint

You can paint anything you want!
You can change color and size, and also use the eraser or clear the whole canvas.
After you're done, just press the Download button!

About KuuppaBrowser

You can browse all websites that allow being in an iframe.
Just type in the URL (like 'wikipedia.org') and press load!
You can even have KuuppaOS in KuuppaOS!

About Calculator

This is just a basic calculator.
Use the buttons or just type your numbers in the textarea and press solve!

About Pong

You can play pong.
Use your mouse to move. It's that simple!
You can pause by minimizing the app. Reset by closing it.

About Camera

Take pictures inside KuuppaOS.
Just press the Take Photo button!
Then you will see the photo for a few seconds and then your download will start.

About Ghost game

Pacman like game where you try to eat all the ghosts.
Also everyone can see the best score if you get it!
I made this a while back in a coding class.

About About

You can see info about the apps in KuuppaOS!

About Welcome

This is just the welcome screen with some links.
